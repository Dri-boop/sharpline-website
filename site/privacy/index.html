Privacy policy

How we handle your personal information, in plain language, as required by the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who we are

Sharpline Renovation & Woodworks, Unit 4, 7th Avenue Business Park, Maitland, Cape Town. Our Information Officer is the owner, Charles Zeka. Contact us on WhatsApp or by phone: +27 67 617 7735.

What we collect

  • What you send us. When you WhatsApp us, call us or use the quote form, we receive what you share: usually your name, phone number, suburb, project details and photos of the space.
  • Website statistics. If you click OK on the cookie notice, Google Analytics records how the site is used: pages viewed, the type of device and browser, your approximate area (city level) and how you found us (for example a Google search). It also counts clicks on our WhatsApp and phone buttons and quote form sends. We do not see your name or phone number in these statistics.

The quote form does not store your details on this website. It opens WhatsApp with your message ready, and you choose to send it.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you, visit, measure, quote and do the work you ask for.
  • To keep records we need for our business and for tax.
  • To understand which pages help people, so we can improve the website.

We never sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • WhatsApp (Meta), when you message us on WhatsApp.
  • Google, for Google Analytics statistics (only if you click OK).
  • GitHub, which hosts this website and keeps normal server logs.

These companies may store data outside South Africa, under their own privacy and security rules.

Cookies

We only use Google Analytics cookies (named _ga and _ga_…), and only after you click OK. If you click No thanks, no analytics cookies are set; Google may still receive a basic, cookie-free signal that a page was viewed. We do not use advertising cookies. Your choice is saved in your browser.

How long we keep it

We keep quote and project details for as long as we need them for the job and for our business and tax records. Google Analytics statistics are deleted automatically after 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. You can also object to how we use it. Just contact us on WhatsApp or by phone. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za.

Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and only share it with the people who need it to do your job.
