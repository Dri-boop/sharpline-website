Working in Durbanville

Durbanville sits at the edge of the Northern Suburbs, near the Durbanville wine valley, with newer family homes and security estates, many with pools, double garages and entertainment areas. Summers are hot and dry, so outdoor timber needs protection from the sun. Estates often have design rules for outside changes. Family kitchens, built-in cupboards, garage doors and pool decks are common requests.
