Working in Thornton

Thornton is a small, quiet suburb between Pinelands and Epping, with freestanding family homes on decent plots, garages and established gardens. Many houses date from the middle of the last century and have been extended over the years, so floor levels, ceiling heights and window styles can change from room to room. Common work here is replacing original kitchens and wardrobes, upgrading front and garage doors, and building storage into extensions so they feel part of the house.
