Working in Goodwood

Goodwood runs along Voortrekker Road near the showgrounds, with family homes and semi-detached houses, many from the early and middle 1900s. Lots of young families are buying and renovating here, so kitchens, bathrooms and bedrooms are being modernised. Many homes also need new front doors, gates and garage doors. It is only a short drive from our Maitland workshop.
