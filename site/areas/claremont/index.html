Working in Claremont

Claremont is a busy Southern Suburbs centre around Cavendish Square, with family homes on its quieter streets, many townhouse complexes and apartment blocks. Homes in complexes often come with rules about outside changes and working hours. Upper Claremont has larger houses on bigger plots. Common work here is modern kitchens, built-in wardrobes and storage for townhouses where space is planned tightly.
