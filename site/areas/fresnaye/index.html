Working in Fresnaye

Fresnaye sits on the slopes between Sea Point and Lion's Head, with mostly large homes on steep plots, many with pools, terraces and big windows facing the sea. Homes here are often built for entertaining, with large kitchens, sculleries and bars. The combination of sun, salt air and wind is hard on outdoor timber and hardware, so materials need to be chosen with care and maintained.

Do you do high-end kitchens in Fresnaye?

How often does a pool deck in Fresnaye need sealing?

Usually once or twice a year, depending on sun exposure. We'll show you how to keep it in good condition.

Yes. We offer sprayed lacquer, timber veneer and high-quality board finishes, depending on the look and durability you want.
