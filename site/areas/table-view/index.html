Working in Table View

Table View faces Table Mountain across the bay, with family homes close to the beach and plenty of townhouse complexes. It is known for strong summer wind, which brings sand and salt into everything. Doors, windows, garage doors and outdoor timber here need sturdy, rust-resistant hardware and regular care. Kitchens and built-in cupboards are the most common indoor upgrades.

Questions from Table View

What doors cope best with the wind in Table View?

Solid, well-fitted doors with good seals; for garages, aluminium works well near the coast. We'll recommend what suits your home.

How do I keep sand and wind out around my doors?

Well-fitted frames, good seals and solid hardware make a big difference in Table View's wind.

Will a timber garage door survive near the beach?

With the right timber, finish and stainless hardware, yes, but it needs regular maintenance. Aluminium is a low-maintenance option.
