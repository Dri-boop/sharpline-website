Working in Tamboerskloof

Tamboerskloof climbs the slopes below Lion's Head and Kloof Nek, with steep streets of Victorian houses, many with stoeps, balconies and terraces looking over the city. Plots are small and often terraced into the hill, so decks and outdoor living spaces are a big part of many homes. Plenty of houses are let as short-term rentals, which need tough, easy-to-clean finishes and fast turnarounds. Access on steep, narrow streets is part of planning any job here.
