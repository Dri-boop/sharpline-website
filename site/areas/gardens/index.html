Working in Gardens

Gardens takes its name from the Company's Garden and runs up from the city towards Kloof Nek. It has Victorian and Edwardian houses with high ceilings, timber floors and sash windows, often right next to apartment blocks and townhouses. Around Kloof Street many homes are flats or rentals. In the older houses, original doors, floors and windows are worth saving, while in the flats the aim is to make every centimetre of storage count.

Questions from Gardens

Can you match existing woodwork in an older Gardens home?

Yes. We can match profiles and finishes of existing doors, skirtings and cupboards, so new work sits naturally with the original.

Can you restore original timber doors in a Gardens house?

Yes. We repair damaged panels and frames, refit hinges and locks, and strip or repaint as needed.

Can you fit a wardrobe wall in a small apartment?

Yes. A wall of sliding-door wardrobes up to the ceiling gives the most storage for the floor space it takes.

Do you make integrated appliance kitchens?

Yes. We build kitchens around integrated fridges, dishwashers and ovens, following the appliance manufacturer's fitting dimensions.
