Working in Brooklyn

Brooklyn sits between Rugby, Ysterplaat and Milnerton, close to the N1 and Koeberg Road. Homes here are mostly older, smaller houses on compact plots, many with additions and backyard rooms. With limited space, good storage matters: our Brooklyn photo shows a compact kitchen with a tall pantry unit that uses the full height of the wall. Solid front doors, security gates and garage doors are other common upgrades in the area.

Questions from Brooklyn

Do you work in Brooklyn and Rugby?

Yes. Both are only a few minutes from our Maitland workshop.

How do you get more storage into a small kitchen?

Tall pantry units, drawers instead of doors in the base units and cupboards up to the ceiling all add space without making the room feel smaller.

Can you fit a kitchen around old, uneven walls?

Yes. We measure carefully and scribe end panels and tops to the walls so the finish looks straight.
