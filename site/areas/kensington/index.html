Working in Kensington

Kensington, next to Maitland and Factreton, is an established working-class suburb of family homes, many built small and extended over the decades as families grew. Plots are modest, so additions often go to the back or up, and storage is always short. Typical work is a new kitchen in place of a tired original, built-in wardrobes in bedrooms, and solid front doors and security gates. We are a few minutes away in Maitland, which keeps site visits quick.

Questions from Kensington

How quickly can you come out to Kensington?

Usually within a day or two. Our workshop is just next door in Maitland, and you can send photos on WhatsApp any time for a first idea of the job.

Can you build wardrobes in small bedrooms?

Yes. Sliding doors save floor space, and taking the wardrobe up to the ceiling adds a lot of storage without taking more room.

Do you fit security gates and front doors?

Yes. We fit solid timber and steel front doors and security gates, measured to your openings.

Can you replace a front door without changing the frame?

Often, yes. If the frame is sound and square, a new door can be fitted to it; if not, we recommend replacing the frame as well.
