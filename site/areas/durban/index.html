Working in Durban

Durban has a humid, subtropical climate, very different from Cape Town's, and timber moves more in humidity. That affects how furniture and joinery should be built and finished. Our Durban photos show pine bookcases and a pine serving stand built for clients there. For projects outside the Western Cape, we agree measurements, materials and timelines upfront, build in our Maitland workshop and arrange delivery.

Questions from Durban

Do you really work in Durban?

Yes. We've built bookcases and furniture for clients in Durban. For projects outside the Western Cape, send us the details and we'll tell you how we can help.

How do you deliver furniture to Durban?

We build it in our Maitland workshop and arrange delivery. Larger pieces are made to come apart for transport.

Does Durban's humidity affect timber furniture?

Yes. We allow for timber movement in the design and use finishes that seal the wood well.

How do you measure for a project so far away?

For furniture, your measurements and photos are usually enough. For installed work, we plan a site visit as part of the project.
