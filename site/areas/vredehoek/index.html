Working in Vredehoek

Vredehoek lies on the slopes of Devil's Peak above the city, along De Waal Drive. It mixes apartment blocks with family homes, and it is one of the windiest parts of the City Bowl when the south-easter blows. That wind tests doors, windows, gates and anything built outside, so hinges, seals and fixings need to be solid. In the apartments, compact kitchens and built-in storage are the usual requests.

Questions from Vredehoek

Do you work in apartment blocks in Vredehoek?

Yes. We coordinate with body corporates on access and noise times, and build in our workshop so installation is quick.

Can you make doors that don't rattle in the south-easter?

Yes. Well-fitted frames, good seals and quality hinges and locks stop most rattling and draughts.

Do you do kitchens in Vredehoek apartments?

Yes. We measure the space, build in our workshop and install with minimum time in the building.

Do you get body corporate approval for us?

We provide the details the body corporate usually needs, such as the scope of work, timeline and contractor information, and follow their rules on site.
