Every job is different, so every price is worked out for your space, your materials and your finishes. Here's how you get from a photo to a fixed price.

  1. 01

    Send your request, 24/7

    WhatsApp us photos and rough measurements, or fill in the quote form, day or night.

  2. 02

    Site visit & measure

    We come out, measure properly and talk through materials and finishes with you.

  3. 03

    Written, itemised quote

    You get every material, finish and cost listed, plus the timeline. No vague totals.

  4. 04

    Price locked

    Once you accept the quote, the price is fixed and work is scheduled.

Charles ZekaFounder · 15+ years in the trade

Charles learned his trade in Malawi, where he ran his own workshop, and has more than 15 years of experience in carpentry and renovation. He started Sharpline Renovation & Woodworks in Cape Town on one simple rule: do the right job for the client, and don't leave until the client is happy.

  • 15+years of experience
  • 4permanent team members
  • 1fixed price, no surprises

Today Sharpline is a team of four permanent craftsmen, with trusted specialist contractors brought in for bigger jobs. Units are built in our own workshop in Maitland, central to the City Bowl, the Southern and the Northern Suburbs, and we take on projects anywhere in South Africa. Clients come back, and recommend us to their friends, because we do what we say, at the price we quoted.
